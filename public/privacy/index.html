Privacy Policy

Last updated October 9, 2026

This policy explains what information DJKMD Presents Legends ("Legends", "we") collects when you visit djkmdlegends.com, buy tickets, call us or join our mailing list, and what we do with it. The short version: we collect only what we need to sell you tickets, seat your party and tell you about upcoming shows. We never sell your information.

What we collect

  • When you buy tickets (online, by phone or at the door): your name, email address, phone number, the show, ticket type, number of tickets and your table or seats.
  • Payment details are handled by our payment processor, Square. We never see or store your full card number.
  • When you join our mailing list: your name and email address, and how you signed up.
  • When you follow one of our links (from an email, a poster QR code or an ad): a record that the link was used, which link it was (for example "email" or "facebook"), the time, the website that sent you, your approximate country and your browser type. We use this to learn which of our announcements work.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your information to anyone.
  • We don't use advertising cookies or tracking pixels, and we don't follow you around other websites.

How we use it

  • To process your tickets, hold your seats and seat your party together.
  • To contact you about your booking, for example if a show's time or venue changes.
  • To send you news about upcoming shows if you're on our mailing list. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link, and if you unsubscribe we stop.
  • To understand which of our announcements bring people to the site.

Who helps us

We use a few trusted service providers to run the business. They process information only on our behalf: Square (payments), Mailgun (sending email) and Cloudflare (hosting this website). We may also share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep booking records as long as we need them for the show and for our business and tax records. We keep mailing-list details until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. If you unsubscribe, we keep a note of it so you aren't added back by mistake.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from our emails at any time with the link at the bottom of every email. To see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, email booking@djkmdlegends.com or call 844-DJKMD-44, and we'll take care of it.

Children

Our shows and this website are meant for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
